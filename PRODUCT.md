# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: experienced Malaysian entrepreneurs (SPM-qualified, years running real businesses) and organisational leaders (NGOs, cooperatives, associations, consultancies, senior administration) — mostly 30s–50s, mobile-first, BM-speaking, professionally accomplished but formally under-qualified. They arrive asking: "Can someone with my experience earn a Diploma without abandoning my business/role, and will someone guide me?"

Secondary: enrolled participants checking progress; the KOBIS/Coach Roszie team managing applicants; the Programme Director reviewing numbers and simple finance.

## Product Purpose

SPM2Diploma 2.0 is a high-conversion recruitment website for two Malaysian Skills qualifications (DKM Level 4 Keusahawanan, DLKM Level 5 Kepimpinan Korporat) run by KOBIS Berhad with Coach Roszie as Operating Partner and Lead Trainer. Its single commercial job: convert serious, qualified, financially prepared applicants into paid participants. It deliberately uses a hybrid model — the site handles marketing, qualification (Semakan Kelayakan), applicant records, participant progress visibility and simple management oversight; coaching, document review, WhatsApp communication and operations stay human-led. Success = qualified committed applications that enrol, not raw lead volume.

## Brand Personality

Dignified, guided, earned. The voice of a respected mentor who takes your experience seriously — never a discount education ad, never shaming, never hype. Emotional goals: recognition ("my experience counts"), aspiration ("this is my next level"), reassurance ("I will not walk this alone"). Malay-first warmth with professional gravity.

## Anti-references

- Cheap education-ad look: graduation-cap clip art, loud gradients, neon, fake countdowns, artificial scarcity, confetti, gamification, emoji-heavy copy.
- "Fast track / instant diploma" promise sites — V1's "3–6 bulan fast track" framing is explicitly removed.
- Shaming copy ("tak perlu malu", "you are left behind").
- Enterprise-dashboard maximalism: chart walls, CRM automation feel.
- Generic foreign-student stock imagery; anything that doesn't look Malaysian/Sarawak professional.

## Design Principles

1. **Desire before bureaucracy.** Aspiration, pathways, human guidance first; JPK/NOSS/TVET terminology only after trust is built, in expandable layers.
2. **The guide is the proof.** Coach Roszie + KOBIS team are the trust engine; every conversion moment carries the journey-together message.
3. **Filter, don't harvest.** The 5–8 minute Semakan is a commitment device — friction is a feature that selects serious candidates.
4. **Truth is editable.** Official names, NOSS refs, fees, durations live in marked editable config; nothing official is invented, nothing is guaranteed.
5. **Proportionate always.** A simple, reliable, beautiful feature beats a complicated unfinished module; manual/WhatsApp workflows are respected, not replaced.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA intent: ≥4.5:1 body contrast, large mobile-friendly controls, clear validation, keyboard-navigable forms and dashboards, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured. Four languages (BM default, EN, 中文, Iban) with persistent selection; ZH/Iban strings flagged for human verification. Floating tools (WhatsApp, Digital Officer, sticky CTA) must never obstruct content or each other on small screens.
